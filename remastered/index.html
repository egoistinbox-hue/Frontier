This page does no per-frame readbacks and no CPU geometry work in the render loop.
Why this is fast: static buffers, 2 passes max, tiny uniform updates, no mapAsync / readbacks in the hot loop, DPR capped at 2. If you still see low FPS, look at device selection, resolution, or shadow size first — not the lighting formula.
